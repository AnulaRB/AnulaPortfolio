Projects

Mentra

Mentra is a comprehensive ML-driven platform designed to bridge the gap between academic performance and student well-being. By integrating academic tracking with predictive mental health support, the system utilizes advanced machine learning models—including Naive Bayes, Decision Trees, and time-series analysis—to detect subtle behavioral and emotional patterns. It effectively analyzes mood data to provide early intervention, generating real-time alerts for counselors when behavioral or academic indicators suggest a student may need support.

AbhyasPay

AbhyasPay is a digital payment confidence platform specifically engineered to help older adults and first-time users overcome the fear of online scams. The platform features a unique "Live Guide" overlay that provides real-time transaction assistance, along with a risk-free simulation mode to practice using apps like PhonePe without risking real money. Additionally, it integrates a Gen AI voice assistant to conduct safety drills and uses a reward economy with brand coupons to incentivize secure digital habits.

Intranet

The Intranet Deployer is a localized hosting platform developed for the MMCOE private network to manage internal applications and academic resources. It provides students and faculty with seamless access to campus-specific tools without requiring external internet, ensuring data privacy and efficient bandwidth usage. This centralized hub streamlines the deployment of college projects and administrative services within a secure local infrastructure.
